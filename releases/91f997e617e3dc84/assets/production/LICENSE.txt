The explorer and collector body and rig are adaptations of Rain v3.3. Changes include winter
clothing, gloves, gaiters, webbing, hip carrier, UV atlases, PBR baking, a reduced
43-bone deformation rig, game-unit conversion, and new locomotion/attack clips.
This is not an endorsement by Blender Foundation or Blender Studio.
Rain-derived character mesh and texture adaptations retain CC BY 4.0 attribution.
The v22 portrait replaces the Rain-derived stylized face and hair with a fitted
MakeHuman hm08 head, anatomical eyes, eyebrow004 and eyelashes03 (CC0 1.0).
MakeHuman Community: https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/makehuman_system_assets.html
The head uses CC0 shape targets from the pinned MakeHuman source revision
recorded in portrait-sources.json. Facial proportions and material grading
are adapted for the explorer. No endorsement by MakeHuman is implied.

The skin color, derivative chestnut hair albedo and ivory shearling material were generated with OpenAI image
generation for this project; the skin uses the existing MakeHuman UV layout.
They are UV-mapped and baked onto the actual 3D surfaces, not portrait overlays.
The hood and fur are original adaptations. This does not establish facial
identity with the generated key art. Optional geometry-only LODs share the
same portrait UVs and textures.
