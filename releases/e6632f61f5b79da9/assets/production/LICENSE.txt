The explorer and collector body and rig are adaptations of Rain v3.3. Changes include winter
clothing, gloves, gaiters, webbing, hip carrier, UV atlases, PBR baking, a reduced
43-bone deformation rig, game-unit conversion, and new locomotion/attack clips.
This is not an endorsement by Blender Foundation or Blender Studio.
Rain-derived character mesh and texture adaptations retain CC BY 4.0 attribution.
The v21 portrait replaces the v20 anatomical head and procedural strand groom
with Rain's authored facial topology, fitted eyes, eyelids and sculpted hair.
Changes include warm skin grading, cheek and lip color, hazel irises, softer
brows, portrait proportions, side-swept fringe and loose face-framing locks.
The hood and fur are original adaptations. This does not establish facial
identity with the generated key art. Optional geometry-only LODs share the
same portrait UVs and textures.
The game source code is not licensed by this asset credit.
